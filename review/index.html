Try Sprint OS on your phone

Nothing to install from a shop, no password, no sign in. It opens like a web page and then sits on the home screen like an app. Everything in it is made up example data, so tap anything you like.

To keep it on the home screen, on an iPhone

  1. It must be open in Safari, not in Chrome and not inside WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the share button, the square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down that list and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Close Safari. There is now a Sprint icon with the cheetah on the home screen. Open it from there and the address bar is gone, so it looks and feels like an app.
