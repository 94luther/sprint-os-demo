Sprint OS: what to build next

Researched against what is already on this machine. 15 September 2026, for Luther's review.

The short answer

I looked for existing tools that do what Sprint OS does, then measured them against what is already built. Most of what the trend is selling, this system already has. Bento grid was in the first version of this page and you took it out, so it is gone from every list below.

Read this before agreeing to anything. Fleetbase would look like a shortcut. It is the opposite. Its free licence requires anyone running it as a service to publish their own source, and Sprint's rate logic and customer terms sit in that code. The paid licence starts at more than two thousand five hundred US dollars every month. Sprint OS is already further along in the ways that matter here: proof at the door, the incident ladder, the expiry wall and the tender countdown are all built and none of them come in that box.

What this does not cover

  • NOW, youThis assumes the app in question is Sprint OS, the office system your mother sits with on 30 September. The customer delivery app is a separate product with a separate wall, and nothing here touches it. If you meant that one, say so before phase 1 starts.
  • HARDWARE, youNothing in this research has been tested on an iPhone. The scanning finding comes from Apple's own record of what Safari supports, not from a phone in this building.
  • NOW, meNo figure here is a measurement of speed on a real phone. Every percentage is a judgement of how much of a capability already exists, not a benchmark.
  • NOW, meDirector sweep: none of these six change what the MD can see. Cost per person by name, margin per customer and what leaks unbilled are still absent, and a road engine in phase 2 is the first thing here that would start answering cost per delivery.
  • HARDWARE, youA road engine needs the map of Botswana loaded once and about two gigabytes of memory. That is a decision about the office machine, not a spend.
